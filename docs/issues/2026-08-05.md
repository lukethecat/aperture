  > Today's thread centers on open weights growing up — and the friction that comes with it. Mistral's Shieldstral pushes moderation into open, multimodal territory, giving the ecosystem a dedicated safety model rather than a bolt-on filter, while a ternary 20B MoE hitting 120 tokens per second on an iPhone shows how far on-device inference has come: serious models are leaving the data center. But the measurement layer is wobbling. Money is moving as fast as models: Google's maneuver to shift billions in Anthropic chip commitments off its balance sheet illustrates the financial engineering now underwriting Big Tech's AI infrastructure bets. On the generative media front, MiniMax H3 becoming the first open model to top a major video ranking underscores how quickly Chinese open-source video models are setting the pace, with Alibaba's latest Qwen release reinforcing that momentum on the agentic side. Rounding out the day, a practical guide to securing AI agents, MCP servers, and LLM applications in production signals the industry's shift from demos to deployment discipline — the attack surface is real, and the tooling to defend it is finally catching up.
